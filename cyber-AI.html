Home Ethics Cybersecurity & AI

Ethical Issue 1: Protecting Project Data

Civil engineers work with private project files, building plans, client information, and infrastructure data. If this information is not protected, hackers could steal or misuse it. My values of responsibility and integrity would guide me to follow cybersecurity rules, use strong passwords, and protect sensitive information.

Ethical Issue 2: Over-Relying on AI

AI can help engineers make calculations, review designs, and predict problems. However, it can also make mistakes. My values of safety and responsibility would guide me to always check AI results and not depend on technology without human review.

How My Values Shape My Decisions

My values help me make ethical choices by reminding me that engineering work affects real people. In civil engineering, being honest, responsible, and focused on safety is necessary to protect the public and complete quality work.

My Personal Values

Home Ethics Cybersecurity & AI

Integrity

Integrity means being honest and doing the right thing even when no one is watching. This value comes from my family, school, and work experiences.

Responsibility

Responsibility means taking ownership of my actions and completing tasks the right way. This is important because engineering decisions can affect public safety.

Teamwork

Teamwork means working well with others and respecting different ideas. Civil engineering projects require many people to work together.

Safety

Safety means protecting people from harm. This value is important because engineers must design projects that people can trust.

Ramifications

If these values are not followed, people could lose trust, projects could fail, private information could be exposed, and public safety could be placed at risk.

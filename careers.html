Where Civil Engineering Can Take You

Civil engineers can work for private consulting firms, construction companies, government agencies, transportation departments, utilities, and many other organizations.

Common Job Titles

  • Project Engineer
  • Site / Land Development Engineer
  • Structural Engineer
  • Transportation Engineer
  • Water Resources Engineer
  • Construction Engineer

Skills employers value

Problem solving, communication, teamwork, engineering software, technical writing, and attention to detail.

Where Civil Engineering Can Take You

Civil engineers can work for private consulting firms, construction companies, government agencies, transportation departments, utilities, and many other organizations.

Common Job Titles

  • Project Engineer

    Coordinates project tasks, schedules, documentation and communication between a project team.

  • Site / Land Development Engineer

    Plans site grading, access, drainage and utilities for land development projects.

  • Structural Engineer

    Analyzes and designs structures that safely resist loads.

  • Transportation Engineer

    Plans and improves road, traffic and transportation systems.

  • Water Resources Engineer

    Works on drainage, stormwater, water supply and flood management.

  • Construction Engineer

    Coordinates engineering activities and construction processes to help deliver a project safely.

Skills employers value

Problem solving, communication, teamwork, engineering software, technical writing, and attention to detail.

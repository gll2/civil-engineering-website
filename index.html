What Do Civil Engineers Do?

Civil engineering combines math, science, technology, and problem solving to create infrastructure that supports everyday life.

Design

Create plans for roads, buildings, bridges, drainage systems, and other infrastructure.

Analyze

Use engineering calculations and software to make sure structures and systems are safe.

Improve

Help communities grow by improving transportation, water, and public infrastructure.

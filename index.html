Industry of Interest

Civil engineering is the industry that focuses on designing, building, and maintaining infrastructure such as roads, bridges, buildings, water systems, and transportation systems.

Career Interest

A career that interests me is structural engineering. Structural engineers design and analyze buildings, bridges, and other structures to make sure they are safe, stable, and reliable.

Cybersecurity and AI Impact

Cybersecurity impacts civil engineering because engineers use digital drawings, project files, sensors, and construction data that must be protected. AI also impacts the career because it can help engineers design structures, predict problems, and improve safety. However, engineers must use AI carefully and check its results.

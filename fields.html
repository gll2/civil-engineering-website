Major Fields of Civil Engineering

Civil engineering includes several specialties. Each one focuses on a different part of the built environment.

Structural Engineering

Focuses on buildings, bridges, and other structures that must safely support loads.

Transportation Engineering

Plans and improves roads, highways, traffic systems, and public transportation.

Geotechnical Engineering

Studies soil and foundations to support safe construction.

Water Resources

Works with drainage, stormwater, water supply, rivers, and flood control.

Environmental Engineering

Protects public health and the environment through water and waste systems.

Land Development

Combines site planning, grading, utilities, drainage, roads, and permitting for new developments.

Why it matters

Every specialty works together to make communities safer, more efficient, and more sustainable.
